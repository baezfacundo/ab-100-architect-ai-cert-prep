## Domain Breakdown

The following is a condensed revision checklist. Use the [Microsoft study guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ab-100) for the complete objectives.

### 1. Planning

- Assess business needs, data readiness, and grounding quality.
- Define AI adoption strategy, multi-agent architecture, and prompt guidelines.
- Compare building, buying, or extending solutions; assess ROI, ownership costs, and model routing.

### 2. Design

- Select agent patterns, orchestration, topics, fallbacks, and knowledge sources.
- Design extensibility through Copilot Studio, Microsoft 365, Foundry, and MCP.
- Integrate prebuilt agents and AI capabilities across Dynamics 365 and Power Platform.

### 3. Deployment

- Define monitoring, telemetry, tuning, and testing criteria.
- Plan ALM for agents, connectors, models, and data.
- Address responsible AI, security, governance, residency, access controls, and auditability.

## Study Tips

Suggested preparation sequence:

1. **Map the objectives.** Turn the official study guide into a checklist and mark unfamiliar topics.
2. **Complete the learning path.** Keep short notes explaining when you would choose each architecture option.
3. **Work through a business scenario.** Document requirements, data sources, agent responsibilities, integrations, evaluation criteria, and release controls.
4. **Take the official assessment.** Record weak areas and return to the relevant documentation.
5. **Add third-party practice.** Mix topic-focused review with timed sessions and case studies.
6. **Review your reasoning.** Explain why the selected option satisfies the requirements and why the alternatives fall short.

For each missed question, record the domain, your reasoning, the supporting Microsoft reference, and a short takeaway. Recheck the exam objectives shortly before your exam.
